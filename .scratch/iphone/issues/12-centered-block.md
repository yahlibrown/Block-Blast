# 12: Top band, Board and Tray centered as one block

**What to build:** Replaces ticket 10's split. On tall screens, such as the iPhone Home Screen app, the top band (Score, Combo Tag, Best Score, settings button), the Board and the Tray move as one block. The block is centered with equal space above and below. The Board and Tray stay one margin apart, as on a short screen. Short screens (a Safari tab, the iPhone SE) are unchanged: the band stays at the top.

Found in the second playtest: ticket 10 left a gap between the Board and the Tray, and the top looked stuck up.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** implemented (awaiting phone playtest)
